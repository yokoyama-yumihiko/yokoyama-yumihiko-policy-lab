# Yokoyama Policy Lab
Public Policy Research Platform by Yumihiko Yokoyama. Policy analysis, visualization and public information using AI with cited sources.
## About

Yokoyama Policy Lab is an open public policy research platform.

It aims to explain public policy in a clear, evidence-based, and visually understandable manner.

Research is conducted using official documents, academic papers, government reports, parliamentary materials, and AI-assisted analysis.
